Jamie eBay Dashboard Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Jamie eBay Dashboard is a private personal application used by the account owner to view information from their own eBay seller account.

Information Accessed

The application may access eBay information needed to display listings, orders, sales activity, offers, and seller statistics.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only for the account owner's private dashboard. It is not sold, rented, or shared with unrelated third parties.

Data Storage

Limited dashboard data may be temporarily stored in local cache files on the account owner's private computer.

Account Deletion

The application supports eBay Marketplace Account Deletion notification requirements.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect eBay account information and application credentials from unauthorized access.
